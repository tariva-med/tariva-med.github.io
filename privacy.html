Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains what information the TARIVA MED app and website collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. "TARIVA MED", "we" and "us" mean the team that runs the app. If you have questions, email fptariah03@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your account

  • Your name and email address, and your password if you sign up with email (stored only in scrambled, hashed form).
  • If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email address from them. We don't receive your Google or Apple password.
  • An account ID we create for you (for example TM-3CB2BC).

How you use the app

  • Your settings (text size, appearance, colour theme, font and assistant options).
  • Your bookmarks, highlights and saved answers.
  • The questions you ask the AI assistant, its answers, and which notes it used.
  • Your plan (free or Premium) and your AI credit balance and history.

Voice

  • Listening for "Hey TARIVA" happens on your phone. Audio is not sent anywhere while the app waits for the wake phrase.
  • After the wake phrase or when you tap the microphone, your phone's own speech service (for example from Google or Samsung) turns your speech into text, under that provider's privacy terms. We receive only the text of your question, never recordings.
  • Voice features are optional and can be turned off in Settings.

Payments

Subscriptions and credit purchases are handled by Google Play, the Apple App Store or our payment provider. We receive confirmation of what you bought and when, but never your card or bank details.

Technical information

Our service providers keep standard logs (such as IP address, device type and times of requests) to run the service securely.

How we use it

  • To provide the app: your account, notes, settings, bookmarks, highlights and plan.
  • To answer your questions with the AI assistant, and to keep conversation history if you choose.
  • To protect our notes: the app shows a faint watermark with your name and account ID, and blocks screenshots.
  • To improve TARIVA MED, for example by seeing which questions the notes don't yet cover.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse, and to contact you about your account.

We don't sell your information and we don't show ads.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that help us run TARIVA MED, and only for that purpose:

  • Supabase: stores accounts and app data, and handles sign-in.
  • Anthropic: processes each question you ask the AI assistant, together with the relevant note text, to write the answer. Anthropic doesn't use this data to train its models by default under its commercial terms.
  • Google and Apple: when you choose to sign in with them, and for app store purchases.
  • Payment providers: to process purchases made outside the app stores.
  • GitHub: hosts this website.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or of TARIVA MED.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you clear your assistant history in the app, it is removed from your view; we keep the underlying question log to improve the notes and prevent abuse until you delete your account. When you delete your account, we delete your information within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (such as purchase records).

Your choices and rights

  • Change your settings, turn voice features or conversation memory on or off, and remove highlights, bookmarks or history at any time in the app.
  • Ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account by emailing fptariah03@gmail.com.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under data protection laws. Contact us and we'll help.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit, each person's data is protected so only they can access it, and access to our systems is limited. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

TARIVA MED is made for medical students and is not intended for children under 16. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the changes are significant.
